9+ rules

Use the official course/term grading information for exact marks

1 · Retrieval first

Every study block ends with closed-book questions. Passive reading does not count as mastery.

2 · Mixed practice

Near exams, mix topics so your brain must choose the method before calculating.

3 · Error bank

Classify each miss: concept, algebra, interpretation, speed or careless. Repair the cause.

4 · Spaced reattempt

Re-solve important mistakes after 24 hours and again after about 7 days.

5 · PYQ last mile

Use old papers to learn recurring question structures and time management—not to skip theory.

6 · No false confidence

A topic is “mastered” when you can teach it and solve a fresh problem, not when a video feels familiar.
